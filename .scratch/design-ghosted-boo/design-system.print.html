ghosted.boo

Silence is the subject.

Status is what the user sets. Silence is what happened to them. This system gives silence its own scale, its own colour and its own way of fading a card toward the page — and keeps it visually separate from Status, so the two never argue.

One accent hue for the brand and three for meaning, all at the same lightness and chroma, so no status can win an argument by being brighter. Two faces: a high-contrast serif for numbers and headlines, a plain grotesque for everything you actually read.

Every neutral carries the same 285° hue at very low chroma, so the greys read as a cool bone-white paper rather than as switched-off colour.

Never use opacity to dim text. It double-dims over a tinted background and cannot be tuned per surface — that is why the badges and the failure banner currently look muddier than they should. Use ink-muted / ink-faint.

Accents — one brand hue, three semantics
spectre · 298°oklch(.520 .120 298)Brand, Applied, links, focus ring
ember · 62°oklch(.520 .120 62)Interviewing, going cold, partial, excitement
vital · 152°oklch(.520 .120 152)Offer, Coverage you have
rose · 18°oklch(.520 .120 18)Rejected, missing, closing, destructive
spectre-tintoklch(.945 .032 298)The same hue as a fill
ember-tintoklch(.951 .036 68)The same hue as a fill
vital-tintoklch(.949 .036 155)The same hue as a fill
rose-tintoklch(.953 .030 20)The same hue as a fill

All four accents sit at L 0.52 / C 0.12 and differ only in hue, so a green badge and a red badge carry exactly the same visual weight and no Status can win an argument by being brighter.

0.12 is the ceiling, not a preference. It is the most these four hues can hold at that lightness and stay inside sRGB. Push it and the browser silently clips ember and vital, shifting their hue without telling you. Each accent reads at 4.5:1 or better on its own tint.

Requirements

Instrument Serif has one weight. Never let a browser synthesise bold on it — headline emphasis comes from colour and size, never a heavier cut. Public Sans is the workhorse and is metric-compatible enough with the Geist already installed in apps/web/app/layout.tsx that Geist can stay for UI if you would rather ship only the one new font. font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums on body, everywhere — counts must not shuffle as they change.

Card fades toward the page; ghost at 5%.

Silence is counted from the last thing that actually happened, and applies only where the user is waiting on somebody — an Applied or Interviewing Job Application. A Bookmarked one has nobody to hear from; an Offer, a Rejection or a Withdrawal has already been answered. A closing date takes the tag slot when there is no silence to report.

The card fades toward the page as silence grows — it never turns red. Ghosting is an absence, not an error.

Record

App bar 60px, paper-raised, 1px line bottom, 64px gutters. Wordmark left (ghosted in ink, .boo in ink-faint), Your Profile / Track a job / avatar right.

The tally is the statement serif and the app's only piece of first-person arithmetic — "14 tracked. 6 still in the air. 3 have gone quiet on you." The quiet number is spectre; the words around the numbers are ink-faint.

Table view is the same data in a radius-10 wrap with a paper-sunk header. It exists because six columns stop working around fifty Job Applications. The side panel is 400px of the same system one step down: 48px bar, 14px padding, 32px fields — and a field the extension read off the Posting carries a 2.5px spectre left border, so what was read is distinguishable from what was typed.

Voice

Dry, never chirpy — and it stops being funny the moment the user has a problem to solve.

The wit lives here
Nothing out there yet.
Track your first job and the board fills itself in. Or open the extension on a Posting you are already reading and save it from the page.
No sign of it.
No Job Application at that Status carries what you searched for. The filters are doing more work than you meant them to.
Nobody is ignoring you.
Every job you are waiting on has been heard from in the last week. Enjoy it — this is not the usual state of affairs.
And nowhere near here
Could not load the latest Job Applications. The board below is the last good copy, from four minutes ago. Anything you change now may not save.
Deleting this Job Application removes its Requirements, its Coverage and its Analysis. It cannot be undone.
Analysing costs one credit and takes about ten seconds.
Errors, destructive confirmations and anything about money or data say plainly what happened, what state things are in now, and what to press.

Use the domain words from CONTEXT.md in the UI, capitalised as it capitalises them: Posting, Job Application, Status, Profile, Requirement, Coverage, Analysis.

Never imply the user is owed a reply — "Nobody owes you a reply — but it helps to know who is not sending one." The app speaks first at most once a week, in the nudge above the board, and only ever offers to take the user somewhere. It never sets a Status on their behalf — Status is set by the user and never inferred.

Accessibility

ContrastEach accent reads at 4.5:1 or better on its own tint.
Colour is never aloneEvery badge says its reading in words as well — "Going cold · 34d", not an amber dot.
Silence, three waysThe tag's words, the tag's colour, and the card's fade. Any one alone is enough to sort by.
Everything is reachableEvery card and row is a real link or button, focusable, with a visible focus ring.
Drag is an enhancementDrag-to-move on the board sits over a Status select — never the only way to change a Status.
Never opacity for textDimming is a token choice (ink-muted / ink-faint), so it can be tuned per surface.
