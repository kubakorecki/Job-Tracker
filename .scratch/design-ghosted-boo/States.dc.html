Silence is counted from the last thing that actually happened, and it only applies where the user is waiting on somebody — an Applied or Interviewing Job Application. A Bookmarked one has nobody to hear from, and an Offer, a Rejection or a Withdrawal has already been answered. The card fades toward the page rather than turning red: ghosting is an absence, not an error.
